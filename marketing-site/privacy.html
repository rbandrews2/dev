How Work Zone OS handles public-site data.

Work Zone OS collects the data reasonably needed to operate the public website, process checkout requests, deliver requested communications, and improve product performance.

Newsletter submissions, checkout requests, and contact interactions may include personal data such as name, email address, company name, and phone number. That information is used for support, customer communications, and purchase fulfillment.

This site may use cookies and similar technologies for analytics, session continuity, and marketing measurement. Personal information is not sold to third parties.

Data may be processed by essential service providers such as Stripe, Supabase, hosting providers, analytics tools, and communication vendors acting on behalf of Superior Consultation LLC.

Questions or requests about your data may be sent to admin@workzoneos.org.
