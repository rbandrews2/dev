## 🎯 Key Information About Each Feature

### ⏰ Time Clock
Track crew hours with location verification. Features secure time punch, geo-fencing, overtime tracking, and timesheet generation.

### 💬 Messages
Keep teams connected with announcements and messaging. Includes push notifications, read receipts, thread management, and team-wide broadcasts.

### 📋 Forms Hub
7 form types including C85 (work logs), JSA (safety analysis), DVIR (vehicle inspection), incident reports, whistleblower reporting, time-off requests, and custom company forms.

### 🗺️ Navigation
GPS-enabled field navigation with offline maps, real-time tracking, weather integration, hazard overlays, and route optimization.

### ✨ AI Assistant
24/7 operational support powered by AI. Get help with form completion, safety guidance, operational questions, and field training.

### 🔌 App Integrations
Connect with your business tools. Supports OAuth authentication, API keys, webhooks, data synchronization, and integration health monitoring.

### 👥 Organization & Roles
Manage your team with admin and member roles. Features include team invitations, bulk CSV imports, multi-organization support, and audit logging.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is this a fully functional app?**  
A: No, this is a **view-only demo** showcasing features and design. No actual functionality or data processing.

**Q: Can I submit forms or enter data?**  
A: No, the demo is read-only. You can explore and view all features, but cannot interact with forms or databases.

**Q: Does this require any setup?**  
A: No setup required! Just open `index.html` in your browser.

**Q: What about mobile devices?**  
A: Fully responsive! Works great on phones and tablets.

**Q: Can I customize this demo?**  
A: Yes! The HTML file is editable. Feel free to modify colors, text, or structure to suit your needs.

**Q: What browser do I need?**  
A: Any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). HTML5 compatible.
