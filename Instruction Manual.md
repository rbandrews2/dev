## 1. Purpose of This Manual

This manual provides comprehensive instructions for installing, activating, configuring, and operating **Work Zone OS**, a standalone workforce and operations management platform designed for road crews, construction companies, and field-based organizations.

It is intended for:

* Company owners and administrators
* IT installers or technical leads
* Supervisors and operations managers
* Authorized end users

## 2. System Overview

**Work Zone OS** is a self-hosted, feature-rich operations platform that consolidates critical workforce tools into a single system.

Core objectives:

* Centralize daily operational workflows
* Improve accountability and compliance
* Reduce administrative overhead
* Provide real-time visibility into field activity

## 5. Activation & Licensing

### Activation Code Entry

1. Navigate to the **Activation Screen**
2. Enter your 15-character activation code
3. Select **Verify & Activate**

### Validation Rules

* Codes are single-use
* Codes are validated server-side
* Three (3) failed attempts are permitted

### Lockout Policy

After three failed attempts:

* The application enters a locked state
* A lockout message is displayed:

  > “Your app is now locked. Contact customer service.”

Only authorized support can restore access.
